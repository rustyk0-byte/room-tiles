Every room,
one tap away

Room Tiles turns Home Assistant into big, friendly tiles - room by room - on touch panels around your home and on every phone, tablet and computer.

Try it in your browser Get Room Tiles

Free and open source. For Home Assistant 2026.11 and newer.

Made for the way a home is used

What's on and off, open or locked, running or done - at a glance, from across the room. Change it with one tap.

A board for every room

Lights, fans, blinds, locks, thermostats and switches - large tiles you can read from across the room.

Your home as a map

Every floor, every room, and a marker for each device right where it is. Zoom in for more detail.

Appliances that tell you

Your washer, dryer and dishwasher: running, how long is left, and when it's done.

Clean room by room

Tap the rooms on the map you want your robot vacuum to clean next, in the order you want.

Themes

Dark, Light, and a festive Christmas theme - pick one per panel, or for your phone.

Coming soon: make your own theme, or add new ones others share - the theme format is open.

Designed for touch panels and tablets

Put one in the rooms you use most - on the wall or on a desk.

  • Any tablet with a web browser - an old one in a wall mount works fine.
  • Elecrow ESP32 panels: 7", 9" and 10.1", and a 2.1" round one with a turning knob.
  • Install from your browser - plug the panel into your computer and press Install. No coding.
  • Updates itself when you update Room Tiles, while nobody's using it.
  • Desk stands you can 3D-print, for the 7" and 10.1".
