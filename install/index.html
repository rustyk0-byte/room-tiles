Install Room Tiles on a panel

One USB cable and a few minutes. After this, your panel updates through Home Assistant.

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can talk to USB devices). Open it there to install.

1. Find your panel

The size and the version are printed on the back of the board, like V1.2.

Loading…

2. Install

  1. Plug the panel into this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data.
  2. Press Install on your panel above, pick its port, and wait until it's done.
  3. The page may then offer to connect the panel to Wi-Fi - you can do it here, or on the panel (below). On the round panel it first says the USB port has changed: select the port again, or just close the page.

3. Connect it

Did this board run ESPHome before (your own firmware, or Elecrow's)? In Home Assistant, delete its old device first: Settings › Devices & services › ESPHome › the old device › ⋮ › Delete, then unplug the panel and plug it back in. Otherwise Home Assistant offers Reconfigure instead of Add, and the panel stays on "Connecting to Home Assistant…".
  1. The panel asks for its language: tap yours.
  2. The panel shows a Wi-Fi network to join. Join it with your phone: a page opens where you pick your home network. If it doesn't open, go to http://192.168.4.1.
  3. In Home Assistant (with the Room Tiles integration set up), go to Settings › Devices & services. The panel is under Discovered with the name it shows, like Room Tiles Panel a1b2c3: select Add (never Reconfigure).
  4. A minute later the panel has its look and wording - and asks which area to show if its device has none.
